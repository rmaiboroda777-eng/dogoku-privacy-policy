This Privacy Policy explains what information the "Dogoku: Woof Patrol" mobile game ("the Game", "we") collects, how it is used, and what rights you have as a user.

01Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not require account registration and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information directly. The Game has no chat, comments, or other user-generated content features.

02Data Stored Only On Your Device

Your level progress, selected dog skin, and sound, vibration, and language settings are stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us — the Game has no backend server or user database. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

03Data Collected by Advertising Partners

The Game displays ads through the AppLovin MAX mediation platform, which may connect to other third-party ad networks. These partners may automatically collect your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), device type and model, operating system version, IP address (which may be used to estimate an approximate location, such as country or region), and ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks — for ad delivery, personalization, performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

We do not directly control how each individual ad partner processes this data. Please review AppLovin's own privacy policy at applovin.com/privacy.

04Your Advertising Choices

You can limit ad personalization through your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset advertising ID"). Users located in the EU, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent prompt regarding data collection for advertising purposes.

05Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

06In-App Purchases

The Game offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play Billing, Google Play's official payment system. After purchase, interstitial ads are permanently disabled for that Google account, and can be restored on another device via "Restore Purchases". We do not see or store your payment card details — all payment processing is handled by Google Play, not by the app itself.

07Data Security

Because the Game does not collect or store personal data on our own servers, the risk of a data breach on our end is minimal. Data processed by advertising partners is protected under their own respective security policies.

08Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data processed by our advertising partners, and the right to withdraw consent for data processing used for advertising. For requests regarding data processed directly by AppLovin, please use the link in section 3. For any other inquiries, contact us at r.maiboroda777@gmail.com.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
